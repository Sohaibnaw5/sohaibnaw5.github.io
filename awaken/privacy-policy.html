Aura FX — Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

Aura FX (formerly Awaken) adds animated anime effects to a photo you already have and makes it a live wallpaper. This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: your photos never leave your phone, and the only company Aura FX shares anything with is Google, for ads and for the optional Premium purchase.

Your photos stay on your device

When you pick a photo, Aura FX reads it from your device's gallery and processes it entirely on your phone. Cutting the subject out of the background, estimating depth, recognising the scene and rendering the animation all happen locally, using on-device machine learning.

Aura FX has no servers. Your photos and the wallpapers you make are never uploaded, never transmitted, and never seen by the developer or anyone else. They stay in the app's private storage on your device and are deleted when you delete the wallpaper or uninstall the app.

Ready-made wallpapers

When you open the ready-made gallery, the app downloads the current list of Aura FX's own wallpapers, and small previews of any that are new, from our website (sohaibnaw5.github.io, hosted by GitHub Pages). When you open one, it downloads that picture too. As with any web request, GitHub's servers see your IP address; Aura FX sends nothing else, and the picture is then made into a wallpaper on your phone.

Searching Pinterest

If you search Pinterest from Aura FX, the app opens Pinterest's own app or your browser with the words you typed, plus "wallpaper". From then on you are on Pinterest, and Pinterest's privacy policy applies. Aura FX sends Pinterest nothing else and never sees what you do there. The picture you save comes back only when you pick it with Android's photo picker, as described below.

What is stored on your device

  • The wallpapers you create, including the source image and your effect settings.
  • Your app preferences, such as your chosen effects and quality settings.
  • A count of how many wallpapers you have made, used only to decide when to show an ad.
  • Which premium powers you have unlocked.
  • Whether you have bought Premium, so that it keeps working without a connection.

All of this is local. None of it is sent anywhere.

Advertising

Aura FX is free and is paid for by ads. It shows occasional full-screen ads after you create a wallpaper, and optional rewarded videos you can choose to watch to unlock a premium effect. Ads are never shown on the wallpaper itself.

Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show them, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and operating system version, and how you interact with an ad. This data is handled by Google, not by Aura FX — the developer never receives it, and only ever sees anonymous, aggregated earnings figures.

You can read how Google uses this data at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, and Google's own policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ads on your Android device.

Premium

Premium is an optional one-time purchase that removes every ad and unlocks every power, effect and transition. It is bought through Google Play: your payment details go to Google and are never seen by Aura FX or the developer, and Google's Payments privacy notice applies to the payment.

There is no Aura FX account for it and no sign-in. The purchase belongs to the Google account your Play Store uses, and Google Play gives it back if you reinstall Aura FX or move to a new phone. To know whether you own it, the app asks Google Play on your phone and checks Google's signature on the answer, also on your phone. Nothing about your purchase is sent to the developer.

Once you own Premium, Aura FX shows no more ads, and from the next time it opens it no longer starts Google's ads SDK at all.

Your consent choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or a US state with a privacy law that applies, Aura FX asks for your choices about personalised ads the first time you open it, using Google's official consent tool.

You can change those choices at any time: open Aura FX and go to Settings → Ad privacy choices.

What Aura FX does not do

  • No account, sign-in, name, email address or phone number is ever requested.
  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No location data is collected.
  • No contacts, messages, calendar or microphone access.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with any third party other than Google, for ads and purchases. A Pinterest search only opens Pinterest, at your request.

Permissions

  • Photos — Aura FX never asks for access to your photo library. You choose an image using Android's own photo picker, which hands the app that single picture and nothing else. The app cannot see any other photo on your device.
  • Internet and network state — used to load ads, to download the depth model the first time you need it, and to download the ready-made wallpapers.
  • Advertising ID — used by Google AdMob to serve ads, as described above.
  • Google Play billing — lets Google Play sell you Premium and tell the app that you own it.

Aura FX also uses a few routine Android permissions to download that model in the background and to keep the wallpaper rendering. None of them touch personal data, and the app requests no location, contacts, camera or microphone access of any kind.

Children

Aura FX is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect any information from children.
