Kakusei — Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 September 2026

Kakusei turns your own photos into animated live wallpapers. This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: your photos never leave your phone, and the only company Kakusei shares anything with is Google, for ads.

Your photos stay on your device

When you pick a photo, Kakusei reads it from your device's gallery and processes it entirely on your phone. Cutting the subject out of the background, estimating depth, recognising the scene and rendering the animation all happen locally, using on-device machine learning.

Kakusei has no servers. Your photos and the wallpapers you make are never uploaded, never transmitted, and never seen by the developer or anyone else. They stay in the app's private storage on your device and are deleted when you delete the wallpaper or uninstall the app.

What is stored on your device

  • The wallpapers you create, including the source image and your effect settings.
  • Your app preferences, such as your chosen effects and quality settings.
  • A count of how many wallpapers you have made, used only to decide when to show an ad.
  • Which premium powers you have unlocked.

All of this is local. None of it is sent anywhere.

Advertising

Kakusei is free and is paid for by ads. It shows occasional full-screen ads after you create a wallpaper, and optional rewarded videos you can choose to watch to unlock a premium effect. Ads are never shown on the wallpaper itself.

Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show them, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and operating system version, and how you interact with an ad. This data is handled by Google, not by Kakusei — the developer never receives it, and only ever sees anonymous, aggregated earnings figures.

You can read how Google uses this data at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, and Google's own policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ads on your Android device.

Your consent choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or a US state with a privacy law that applies, Kakusei asks for your choices about personalised ads the first time you open it, using Google's official consent tool.

You can change those choices at any time: open Kakusei and go to Settings → Ad privacy choices.

What Kakusei does not do

  • No account, sign-in, name, email address or phone number is ever requested.
  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No location data is collected.
  • No contacts, messages, calendar or microphone access.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with any third party other than Google, for ads.

Permissions

  • Photos — Kakusei never asks for access to your photo library. You choose an image using Android's own photo picker, which hands the app that single picture and nothing else. The app cannot see any other photo on your device.
  • Internet and network state — used to load ads and to download the depth model the first time you need it.
  • Advertising ID — used by Google AdMob to serve ads, as described above.

Kakusei also uses a few routine Android permissions to download that model in the background and to keep the wallpaper rendering. None of them touch personal data, and the app requests no location, contacts, camera or microphone access of any kind.

Children

Kakusei is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
