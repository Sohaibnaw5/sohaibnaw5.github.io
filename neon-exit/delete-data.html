Neon Exit — Delete your data

Neon Exit: Arrow Puzzle · developed by M Sohaib Nawaz

Neon Exit has no sign-up. If you used Play a Friend, the game created an anonymous guest ID and stored match rooms on Google Firebase. You can delete them at any time.

Delete in the game

  1. Open Neon Exit.
  2. Go to Settings → Online → Delete my online data.
  3. Tap Delete. Your guest ID and the match rooms you were in are deleted straight away.

Delete without the game

Email n7638519@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Neon Exit data". If you can, include the guest ID shown in the game under Settings → Online → Delete my online data. Your data is deleted within 30 days and you get a reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • The anonymous guest ID (Firebase Authentication).
  • Your match rooms: the generated player names, boards and match progress (Firebase Realtime Database).

Nothing is kept after deletion.

What this does not affect

  • Your progress, coins and settings are stored only on your phone; uninstalling the game deletes them.
  • Ads data held by Google AdMob is controlled by Google: reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, and see how Google uses this information.
