Neon Exit — Privacy Policy

Neon Exit: Arrow Puzzle · Last updated 2 November 2026

Neon Exit is an arrow puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what the game does with your data. The short version: your progress stays on your phone, Google shows the ads, and if you choose to play a friend live, a small match room is stored on Google Firebase until the match is over.

Your progress stays on your device

Your levels, stars, coins, shop items, daily streak, settings and any ad-free gift code are stored on your phone only. They are never sent to the developer. Uninstalling the game deletes them. If your phone's own Android backup is switched on, Android may copy them to your Google account; that copy is handled by Google under its terms.

No sign-up, no analytics, no in-app purchases. Neon Exit never asks for your name, email address or phone number.

Advertising

Neon Exit is free and is paid for by ads: videos you choose to watch for a reward (more time, an extra heart, double coins, a hint or free coins), and now and then a full-screen ad between levels. Ads never appear in the middle of a level.

Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show them, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location can be worked out), device type and operating system version, and how you interact with ads. This data is handled by Google; the developer only sees anonymous, aggregated earnings figures.

See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Your consent choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or a place whose law requires it, Neon Exit asks for your choices about personalised ads before any ad loads, using Google's consent tool. You can change them at any time in Settings → Ads → Ad privacy choices.

Play a Friend (live duels)

Play a Friend lets you invite a friend with a room code and play a live match. It is used only if you choose to use it. To run the match, the game uses Google Firebase:

  • Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous guest ID for your phone. It is not linked to your name, email address or Google account.
  • Firebase Realtime Database (Google servers in Singapore) stores the match room: its code, both players' names, the three boards, each player's progress during the match (arrows left, hearts, and when a board was cleared or lost), and whether each player is connected.

Only the two players in a room can read it. The data is used only to run the match; it is not used for advertising and is never sold. Firebase also processes your IP address to connect you. A room is deleted when both players have left the match; rooms left behind (for example when a phone was switched off mid-match) are deleted a day or two after they were made.

Player names and reports

You can type a name for Play a Friend or use a generated one (such as "Swift Comet 27"). It is shown only to the friend you play. Names must be friendly: no swearing, hate, personal details or links. The game checks every name before it is used, and there is no chat: your name is the only thing you type that another player sees.

You can hide another player's name, which is kept on your phone, or report it. A report (your guest ID, the reported player's guest ID and name, and the room code) is stored on Google Firebase so the developer can review it, and it is deleted once reviewed. Invites are shared through Android's share sheet; the game never reads your contacts.

Match history

Your duel results are stored on your phone only: the date, the other player's name, the score and the coins won or lost. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Deleting your data

  • In the game: Settings → Online → Delete my online data deletes your guest ID and any match rooms you were in, straight away.
  • Without the game: see Delete your data.
  • Data on your phone: uninstall the game.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and to play Play a Friend.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob to serve ads, as described above.

Neon Exit requests no location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or storage access.

Children

Neon Exit is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Security

Everything sent to Google's services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
